# Front door (signed out)

Scope: `/`. Mode: Persuade. Signed-in visitors never see it (redirect to queue).

Audience: a hiring manager or reviewer with thirty seconds, and an AP operator
arriving for the first time. Action: sign in to the console as a demo role.
Proof: the product's own evidence, a synthetic invoice from the demo data shown
as the console prints it. No customers, numbers or claims that do not exist.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The front door is the run's banner page: it shows one real exception
line, ordered against received against billed, before it says anything about
itself. It refuses the SaaS hero with a screenshot and feature cards.

OWN-WORLD: Same stationery as the console: cool paper, green bands, green
condensed form headings, black mono figures, red ribbon only on the variance.
No cards, no shadows, square corners, double rule under the one total.

STORY: The visitor learns what Threshold does (checks invoices three ways and
routes the rest to the one person who owns the problem), sees it do that on a
labelled specimen, and signs in as the role that owns that problem.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left: the headline, one lede sentence, the sign-in action and
the "start as buyer" line. Right: the specimen excerpt at printout scale, three
banded lines with the agreed and billed prices, the variance in red, and the
routing line "to Buyer, due in 48 h", labelled synthetic.

FORM: Green-bar Report, position 4 of 7, seed 7f6d8970, shared with the console.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
